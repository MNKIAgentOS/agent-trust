covers the code, not the names.

**You may**
- say that your agent, framework or product *uses* or *supports* mnki / Agent Trust, and link to the
  passport of an agent you own;
- display the **compatibility badge** produced by `mnki conformance --level N --badge` for an
  implementation that passes that level's vectors, unmodified and linked to the report;
- display the **passport badge** served from `mnki.com/badge/<public id>.svg` for an agent whose
  organisation opted into public passports — it states exactly what was checked ("identity registered",
  "verified action in the last 30 days", "runtime attested", "revoked") and must not be altered or cached
  in a way that hides a later "revoked" state.

**You may not**
- name a product, package, domain or organisation "mnki", "Agent Trust" or a confusingly similar name,
  or use the mark as your own logo;
- present "verified" as a safety, quality or endorsement claim by MNKI; a badge only reflects the checks
  named on it;
- redistribute modified copies of the packages under the original names (fork under your own name).
